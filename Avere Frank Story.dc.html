Today

The life we chose

Alyssa and I have four daughters. They come to the office and know everyone by name. When we walk down Butler Street, the other store owners wave out the window.

To us, this is a hobby. The late nights, the calls during dinner: it’s the life we chose. And with four daughters, we never had the option of slowing down. We chose this so that you can have beauty.

2024

We bought Manikure

I always knew I wanted to be more of a private equity person than a marketing guy. After making videos for so many people, I learned they didn’t care how nice it looked. They cared whether it brought in sales. When sales are down, marketing is the first thing that gets blamed.

So we learned every side of the business. By 2024 we were comfortable buying another company.

“My vision and big-picture thinking plus Alyssa’s punctuality and logic made a whirlwind.” — Frank, on the partnership
