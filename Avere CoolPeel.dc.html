The CoolPeel

A CoolPeel can only be done with the SmartXide Tetra, DEKA's most advanced CO2 laser. It delivers high energy in extremely short pulses for resurfacing results without the lingering heat that damages surrounding skin. That's why it's called cool.

Why it's cool

CO2 results, days of recovery instead of weeks

Traditional CO2 resurfacing leaves heat behind in the skin, and that heat is what causes long recovery. The Tetra controls its output so the energy is gone almost as soon as it lands.

CoolPeel1 to 2 days
Typical visible recovery. Expect redness like a sunburn and dry, flaky skin as it heals.
