How it works

Fat cells, dissolved

Kybella is a lab-made version of deoxycholic acid, a molecule your body already uses to absorb dietary fat. Injected into the fat under the chin, it breaks down the walls of fat cells. Your body clears them out naturally over the following weeks.

Stubborn chin fat often doesn’t respond to diet or exercise, and it can show up at any weight. Kybella targets just that pocket.

Your treatment

What to expect

Your injector marks a small grid under your chin and places a series of tiny injections. The visit takes about 20 minutes. Expect swelling, tenderness and some numbness for several days; the swelling is a sign the product is working. Most people need two to four sessions spaced at least a month apart.
