Why we work with Cartessa

Cartessa Aesthetics doesn't build its own lasers. It hand-selects best-in-class technology from manufacturers around the world, then brings it to North American practices with training and clinical support.

That means we aren't locked into one brand's lineup. Our hair removal, resurfacing and tattoo lasers each come from the manufacturer that does that job best, mostly Italy's DEKA and Quanta System.
