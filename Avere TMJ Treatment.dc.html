Relief for a tight, clenched jaw

Clenching and grinding overwork the masseter, the strong chewing muscle at the angle of your jaw. Botox relaxes that muscle so it can’t clench as hard, easing jaw pain, morning headaches and wear on your teeth.

Treating the muscle behind the pain

When the masseter is constantly tight, it pulls on the jaw joint and can cause clicking, soreness, headaches and a tired feeling when chewing. A few injections into each masseter calm that tension while leaving normal chewing and speaking intact.

Your appointment

Quick and precise

We feel the masseter as you clench to find the bulk of the muscle, then place several small injections on each side. It takes about 15 minutes, with no downtime. Many clients continue wearing a night guard from their dentist for extra protection.

Results

Easier mornings

Relief usually starts within one to two weeks as the muscle relaxes. Results last about three to four months at first, and often longer with regular treatment as the muscle becomes less overactive. A slimmer lower face is a common bonus.
