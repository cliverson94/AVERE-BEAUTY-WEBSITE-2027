The consultation

Like studying a painting.

Your face is already beautiful. We look closely at anatomy and symmetry, and at how a small change in one place affects the whole picture. You bring the picture in your head. We bring the tools and the medical training to get there safely.

It’s a lot to take in, so we go at your pace. We also talk, laugh and get to know you.
