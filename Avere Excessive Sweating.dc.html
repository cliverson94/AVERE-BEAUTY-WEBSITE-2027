Derm services · Hyperhidrosis

Stop worrying about sweat

Hyperhidrosis is sweating well beyond what your body needs to stay cool. Botox blocks the signal that tells sweat glands to switch on, so a single quick treatment can keep underarms, hands or the scalp noticeably drier for months.

Small injections, big relief

Botox is FDA-approved for severe underarm sweating. We map the active area, then place tiny amounts just under the skin in a grid. There is no downtime, and most clients notice a dramatic drop in sweating within the first week.

Your appointment

Mapped, then treated

We may do a simple starch-iodine test to show exactly where you sweat most. Numbing is applied to sensitive areas like the hands. The injections take about 20 minutes, and you can return to normal activities right away, skipping the gym and deodorant for the rest of the day.

Results

Months of dryness

Sweating drops within two to four days, with full results in about two weeks. Underarm results typically last 4 to 12 months; hands and feet are usually shorter. Most clients book a repeat treatment when they notice sweat returning.
