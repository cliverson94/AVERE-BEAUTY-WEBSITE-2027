Filler that moves when you do

RHA stands for Resilient Hyaluronic Acid. It's built to stretch and recover with your expressions, so results look natural when you smile, talk and laugh, not only when your face is still. There are four strengths, each made for a different depth and area.

What makes it different

Stretch, then spring back

Most fillers are made stiffer to hold shape. RHA uses a gentler manufacturing process that keeps the hyaluronic acid's natural structure, giving a gel that bends with facial movement and returns to its shape. That makes it a strong choice for the parts of your face that move most: around the mouth, the lips and the smile lines.
