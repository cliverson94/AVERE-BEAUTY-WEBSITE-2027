Our Story

We started in one room on Butler Street.

Avere Beauty began in 2018 with Botox appointments in living rooms and hair salons. Today we care for more than 25,000 clients across four Pittsburgh offices. This is how we got here.

How it started

The brand was built for someone else first.

In 2018, Frank ran a marketing company. A local med spa hired him to build their brand, and he went all in: the name, the look and a plan for everything that came after. When it was time to sign, they passed.

Alyssa, an ICU nurse, looked at what he'd made and said, “Wow. Let’s do this.”

The early days

We knew everyone’s name.

We were scared. We’d drive to the office for a $60 appointment, then place a product order for thousands of dollars and pay the rent.

One room in a small town made it personal. We knew every client by name, and every client’s folder.

Why “Avere”

A name that’s a verb.

Most med spa names describe a look. We wanted something pretty that was also an action. Avere is Italian for “to have,” and we liked the movement and harmony in it. The letters also make a symmetrical logo, which didn’t hurt.

The ICU never left

Evaluate, then act.

Alyssa spent more than 15 years as an ICU nurse, including a return to the unit during COVID. That training runs through every appointment. No two are the same, so we assess each face before we treat it.

On a busy day we see 40 patients across four offices, and it moves like a dance. No one’s life is on the line, but to the person in the chair this is the most important thing happening. They get our full attention.

Our medical director

A real doctor, down the road.

Dr. Jason Luciano is a surgeon at UPMC and holds an MBA. He handles intakes, reviews charts and pushes our team to keep up with new research.

He’s available to our staff around the clock, and he comes to the offices in person.

The work we’re proudest of

The corrective cases and the full transformations. Some of these appointments end in tears, the good kind.
