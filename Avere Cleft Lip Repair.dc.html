Lip refinement after cleft repair

Years after a cleft lip repair, many adults still notice an uneven lip border, a thin or notched upper lip or a visible scar. We use precise, non-surgical treatments to restore balance and soften the scar, with care led by our surgeon medical director.

Small, careful changes

A repaired cleft lip often has a slight step in the lip border, uneven volume from side to side and a scar running toward the nose. Each of these can be addressed on its own. We plan treatment in small amounts, check the balance and build from there, so the result looks like your lip, not a different one.

Your consultation

A plan built around your repair

We talk through your surgical history and what bothers you most, then examine the lip at rest and in motion. Scar tissue behaves differently from untreated lip tissue, so we start with conservative amounts and plan a follow-up to refine. Dr. Jason Luciano, our medical director and a UPMC surgeon, oversees our medical protocols.

Results

Balance you can see right away

Filler and threads show results the same day, settling over about two weeks as swelling goes down. Neurotoxin takes effect in one to two weeks. Scar treatments improve gradually over several sessions. Filler typically lasts 6 to 12 months in the lip.
