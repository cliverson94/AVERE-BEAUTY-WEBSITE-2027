How Moveo works

Gradual heat instead of one hard hit

A traditional laser fires one high-energy pulse. That spike is the rubber-band snap you feel. Moveo glides over a small area in repeated passes, building heat in the follicle until it can no longer grow hair, while the skin stays below the point where it hurts.

Two wavelengths

The right light for your hair and skin

755 nm Alexandrite

The gold standard for hair removal. Highly absorbed by melanin, so it works on finer and lighter hair that other lasers miss. Moveo makes it safe beyond the light skin tones Alexandrite was once limited to.

1064 nm Nd:YAG

Penetrates deeper into the skin. Used for darker skin tones, coarse hair and vascular treatments like spider veins and broken capillaries.

A sapphire tip that touches the skin

Most lasers fire through the air and lose energy to reflection. The Moveo handpiece glides directly on the skin through a cooled sapphire tip, so nearly all of the energy is absorbed. Lower settings do the same work, and the cooling keeps you comfortable.
