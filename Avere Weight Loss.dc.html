The medications

Weekly injectionSemaglutideMimics the GLP-1 hormone to slow digestion and signal fullness. Ideal for steady, gradual weight loss. Common side effects are nausea and upset stomach.
Weekly injectionTirzepatideWorks on two pathways, GLP-1 and GIP, for appetite control and metabolic improvement, with greater weight loss potential. Side effects include GI issues and possible low blood sugar.
Oral tabletContraveCombines bupropion and naltrexone to quiet cravings, especially for emotional or habitual eating. Often used for maintenance. Side effects include nausea and dizziness.

GLP-1 receptor agonists, including semaglutide and tirzepatide, are prescribed for weight management alongside a reduced-calorie diet and increased physical activity. They may cause serious side effects, including a risk of thyroid C-cell tumors. Do not use if you or your family have a history of medullary thyroid carcinoma (MTC) or multiple endocrine neoplasia syndrome type 2 (MEN 2). Contrave carries a boxed warning for suicidal thoughts and behaviors. Your provider will review all risks with you.
