How it works

Three steps, repeated every 6 to 8 weeks

01

The pulse

Your provider matches the wavelength to your ink color. The Toro delivers light in trillionths (picosecond) or billionths (nanosecond) of a second.

02

The shatter

The ink absorbs the light and breaks into tiny fragments. The surrounding skin is left largely untouched.

03

The fade

Over the following weeks your immune system clears the fragments. The tattoo lightens between sessions.

Skin type chart

Safe across the full skin tone range

Skin tone is measured on the Fitzpatrick scale, I (lightest) to VI (deepest). Longer wavelengths are absorbed less by the skin's own melanin, which is why 1064 nm and 785 nm are the go-to choices for deeper skin tones.

I II III IV V VI 1064 nm All skin types 785 nm Ideal for IV to VI 532 nm Best for I to III Used with caution
