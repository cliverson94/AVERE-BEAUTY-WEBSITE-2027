The basics

Three kinds of injectables, three different jobs

Neurotoxins

Botox, Dysport, Xeomin, Jeuveau and Daxxify relax the muscles that crease skin. Best for forehead lines, frown lines and crow’s feet.

Results in about 2 weeks · lasts 3–4 months

Dermal fillers

Hyaluronic acid gels that add volume and shape to lips, cheeks, chin, jawline and under-eyes.

Results right away · lasts 6–18 months

Collagen builders

Sculptra and PDO threads help your skin make its own collagen for gradual, natural-looking firmness.

Builds over 2–3 months · lasts up to 2 years
