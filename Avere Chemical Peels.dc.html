How it works

What a peel actually does

As skin ages, cell turnover slows and dead cells build up, which shows as dullness, clogged pores and uneven tone. A professional peel speeds that cycle back up.

01ExfoliateAcids loosen the bonds between damaged surface cells so they shed.
02StimulateThe controlled exfoliation signals skin to boost cell turnover and collagen production.
03RegenerateFresh, even skin comes through, and your home care absorbs better on top of it.

Customization

Boosters and layering

PCA peels are designed to be customized. Your esthetician can add layers of a base peel for more exfoliation, or finish with a retinol booster to plump, brighten and extend results. Boosters are added on top of a peel, not used alone.

Your appointment

Before, during and after

Before
  • Pause retinoids, exfoliating acids and scrubs for several days before. We'll confirm the exact window when you book.
  • Skip waxing, threading and depilatory creams on the treatment area for a week.
  • Avoid sunburn and tanning. Tell us about any cold sores so we can plan ahead.
  • First peel? Start with a gentler formula so we can see how your skin responds.
During
  • A skin analysis and consultation to choose the formula and number of layers.
  • Cleanse and prep to remove oil so the peel applies evenly.
  • The peel goes on in thin layers. Expect warmth, tingling or itching for a few minutes, and we use a fan to keep you comfortable.
  • Finished with soothing hydration and broad-spectrum SPF.
After
  • Some peels leave a light tint or tightness on day one. Leave the solution on as long as we advise.
  • Gentle cleanser, rich moisturizer and SPF 30+ every day. No retinoids or acids until flaking stops.
  • Don't pick or peel flaking skin. Let it shed on its own to avoid marks.
  • Skip heavy sweating, saunas and hot yoga for 48 hours.

Results

Peels work best as a series

One peel gives you a glow. A series is what fades pigment, clears breakouts and smooths texture. Most plans space peels about four to six weeks apart, matching your skin's natural renewal cycle, then move to maintenance.

Great candidates

Anyone with acne or congestion, melasma and sun spots, dark marks left by breakouts, dull or rough texture, enlarged-looking pores, or early fine lines. PCA formulas are made to work across all skin tones, including deeper skin that's more prone to pigmentation.

Let's wait if you

  • Are pregnant or nursing (some ingredients aren't recommended; we'll suggest alternatives)
  • Have taken isotretinoin (Accutane) recently
  • Have an active cold sore, open wound, rash or sunburn in the area
  • Have had laser, waxing or another peel on the area very recently
  • Are allergic to aspirin (salicylic acid) or hydroquinone
