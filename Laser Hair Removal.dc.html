Cutting edge laser tech

Permanent hair reduction

Laser hair removal works by directing an ultra high-powered, quick ray of light to heat up the melanin in the skin. The melanin absorbs and disperses the heat while the moisture in the hair molecule rapidly heats and ruptures the follicle. Over the course of multiple treatments, the follicles are disrupted and prevent hair regrowth.
