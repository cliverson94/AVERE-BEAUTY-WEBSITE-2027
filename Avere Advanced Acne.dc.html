You don’t have to choose

Traditional dermatology can prescribe medication but may stop there. A med spa can resurface or calm skin without addressing underlying inflammation, hormones or bacteria. Our medical team and aesthetic providers work together to treat acne at every level, from the cause to the scars it leaves behind.

Physician-led protocols

A plan for your skin

All plans are supervised by licensed medical providers, including dermatology-trained NPs and RNs. We look at your skin type, severity, lifestyle and history, then investigate and treat the underlying causes rather than only suppressing symptoms.

Integrated care

Clear skin, then healthy skin

Prescriptions and home care get breakouts under control while in-office treatments calm active acne. Once skin is clear, we move to resurfacing and pigment treatments to heal scars and marks. Whether you’re in your teens or your 40s, acne shouldn’t define you.
