A lift without surgery

PDO threads are fine, dissolvable sutures placed under the skin. Some lift and reposition tissue right away; all of them prompt your body to build new collagen as they dissolve. Results show the same day and keep improving for months.

Lift and rebuild

Two results from one treatment

Barbed threads reposition tissue the moment they’re placed, so you leave with a visibly lifted jawline or brow. Over the next three to six months, every thread type triggers your skin’s healing response, laying down new collagen around each thread. By the time the threads dissolve, that collagen is holding the lift in place.

Your appointment

What happens in the chair

Your injector maps your face while you sit upright, marking where tissue has descended and the direction it should be lifted. The area is numbed, then each thread is placed through a tiny entry point with a fine needle or blunt cannula. Barbed threads are gently smoothed upward to set the lift, and the ends are trimmed beneath the skin. Most appointments take 45 to 60 minutes.

After your visit

Healing and results

Expect some swelling, tightness or small bruises for three to seven days. Sleep on your back, avoid wide yawning, chewing gum and facial massage for about two weeks, and skip intense workouts for the first few days. The lift is visible right away and looks more natural once swelling settles. Collagen keeps building for months, and results typically last 12 to 18 months.
