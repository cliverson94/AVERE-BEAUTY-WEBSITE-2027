Why we started

Genuine aesthetics, minus the sterile room

Pittsburgh has other medspas, but none that represented the practice of genuine aesthetics we believed in. Neurotoxins and dermal fillers are powerful tools used to maintain and improve our appearance, and we wanted to provide these resources in a comfortable environment contrary to the sterile room of a traditional medspa. Avere Beauty is a place to go to relax, unwind, and leave feeling new.

Owner | Injector

Alyssa Luciano, RN

With a background of over 15 years in ICU nursing, Alyssa initially explored aesthetics as a personal hobby. Her natural affinity for injection techniques and a deep understanding of facial anatomy quickly revealed her innate talent within the beauty industry. Her dedication to fostering an informed, transparent, and relaxed environment for aesthetic treatments has not only attracted a faithful clientele of over 20,000 patients, but has also led to the remarkable growth of Avere Beauty.

Alyssa has successfully expanded Avere Beauty into a thriving team of 20 talented & dedicated women, all committed to helping others become the most confident versions of themselves. Alyssa lives in Pittsburgh with her 4 daughters, 3 dogs, and many chickens.
