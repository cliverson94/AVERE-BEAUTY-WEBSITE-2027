About Frank

Marketer by trade, operator by choice

Frank has been building things on a computer since he was seven. He started out directing music videos, including work for Taylor Gang and Wiz Khalifa, and helped grow GLD into a brand doing $50 million a year. After time in corporate roles at Highmark and PPG, he went back to running his own marketing company for local medical practices.

In 2019 he and Alyssa Luciano signed Avere’s first lease. Today he leads growth, marketing and strategy across Avere Beauty, Avere Cosmetics, Manikure and Aura Hair Studio, and manages a growing real estate portfolio.
